# Auto Insurance

## Auto insurance is mandatory

Drivers must purchase and maintain auto insurance to legally drive in Pennsylvania; this is called maintaining "financial responsibility" on your vehicles. A lapse in coverage may result in suspension of your vehicle registration. Operating a vehicle without the required insurance may result in a fine, suspension of your driver's license, and forfeiture of your license plate and registration sticker. Restoration fees and proof of insurance must be provided before registration or driving privileges are returned. Insurers are required to report policy terminations to PennDOT, and drivers are responsible for providing proof of continuous coverage.

## Required coverages and minimum limits

- Medical benefits: pays medical bills for you and others covered by your policy. Minimum limit $5,000; higher limits are available.
- Bodily injury liability: pays damages you are liable for if you injure someone. Minimum limit $15,000 per person and $30,000 per accident; higher limits are available.
- Property damage liability: pays for repairs to someone else's property when you are at fault. Minimum limit $5,000; higher limits are available.
- Some companies offer a combined single limit of $35,000, which meets the bodily injury and property damage liability minimums.

## Premium discounts for older adults

Pennsylvania law requires a premium reduction of at least 5% per vehicle on a private passenger policy when all named policyholders are 55 or older and have completed a PennDOT-approved driver improvement course.

## Getting help

The Insurance Department's Consumer Services Bureau can answer insurance questions and help with complaints.
