# Auto Insurance

## What Pennsylvania law requires

Pennsylvania requires every registered vehicle to be insured. The required coverages are medical benefits (first party benefits) with a minimum limit of $5,000, bodily injury liability with a minimum of $15,000 per person and $30,000 per accident, and property damage liability with a minimum of $5,000. Some companies offer a combined single limit of $35,000, which meets the bodily injury liability and property damage liability minimum requirements.

## Choosing more than the minimum

The minimum limits are what the law requires, not what every driver needs. If you cause an accident, you are responsible for damages above your liability limits. Higher liability limits are often available without a large increase in premium.

## Optional coverages

Optional coverages include collision, comprehensive, higher medical benefits, income loss benefits, rental reimbursement, and towing. Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage must be offered to you and can only be rejected in writing.

## Getting help

The Insurance Department's consumer services staff can answer questions about auto insurance and help with complaints about an insurer or agent.
