# Insurance Law Overview

## Financial responsibility requirement

Pennsylvania law requires every vehicle registered in the state to be covered by financial responsibility (liability insurance) at all times. The minimum amounts are $15,000 for injury or death of one person in an accident, $30,000 for injury or death of more than one person in an accident, and $5,000 for damage to property of another person. You must keep proof of insurance in the vehicle.

## Proof of insurance

Acceptable proof includes a copy of an insurance identification card or a copy of the declaration page of an insurance policy. A letter from an insurer must be on official company letterhead, not from an insurance agent. You must show proof of insurance when you register a vehicle.

## Lapse in coverage

Insurance companies must notify PennDOT when a policy is cancelled by the insured or the insurer. A lapse in insurance results in a three-month suspension of your vehicle registration. An exception applies if the lapse was short (generally 30 days or less) and you prove to PennDOT that the vehicle was not operated during the lapse, using Form MV-221, Statement of Non-operation of Vehicle.

## Avoiding suspension when you stop insuring a vehicle

If you stop insuring a vehicle, return the registration plate and registration card to PennDOT. They must be received no more than 30 days after the insurance was cancelled; late returns result in a three-month registration suspension. Surrender the plate whenever insurance is not in effect, even if the vehicle is being stored or repaired.

## Restoring a suspended registration

If your registration is suspended for a lapse, you may in some cases pay a $500 civil penalty plus a restoration fee instead of serving the suspension; this option can be used only once a year. Restoration requires the restoration fee and proof of insurance.
