# Comparing Quotes and Choosing an Agent

## Get several quotes

Get quotes from at least three different insurance companies. Prices for the same coverage can vary widely between companies.

## Ask for identical coverage

When you get quotes, ask for the same coverages and limits and give the same information to each agent or website. If the quotes do not have matching coverages or limits, contact the companies and ask for a revised estimate using matching coverages and limits.

## Independent and captive agents

An independent agent may sell policies from many different companies, so one conversation can produce quotes from several insurers. A captive agent sells insurance for only one company. Getting quotes through both kinds of agents, and directly from insurers online, gives you a broader comparison.

## Liability limits above the minimum

State minimum liability limits are often too low to fully cover you if you cause a serious accident. Most insurers let you buy higher limits of liability coverage, often without much increase in your premium.

## Check the agent and company

Verify that the agent is licensed and the company is authorized to sell insurance in your state through your state insurance department.
