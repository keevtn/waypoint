# Building an Emergency Fund

## Why an emergency fund matters

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected expenses, such as a car repair, an insurance deductible, or a medical bill, or for a drop in income. Without savings, an unexpected expense can push you into high-cost borrowing or missed bills.

## How much to save

There is no single right amount. Look at the unexpected expenses you have had before and how large they were. A starting goal of $500 can cover many small emergencies. Over time, you can work toward covering larger expenses and a few months of essential costs.

## Start small

Even a small amount of savings can provide some financial security. Small, regular deposits add up over time.

## Make saving automatic

Setting up automatic recurring transfers to savings is often one of the easiest ways to save. Decide how much you can set aside each pay period and move it to savings when you are paid, before paying other bills.

## Use windfalls

Consider using all or part of a tax refund or other lump sum to start or grow your emergency savings.
