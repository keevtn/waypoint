# Deductibles

## What a deductible is

A deductible is the amount you pay out of pocket on a claim before your insurance pays. Deductibles apply to collision and comprehensive coverage. You pay the deductible any time you file a claim with your insurance company, even if you are not at fault for the damage.

## Higher deductible, lower premium

Higher policy deductibles mean lower policy premiums. For the same policy, a $1,000 deductible produces a lower premium than a $500 deductible. Typical deductible amounts are $250, $500, and $1,000.

## Choose a deductible you can afford

Having a higher deductible is a good way to save money on your premium and to submit fewer claims. But be sure you can afford the deductible in case you have a loss. If paying the deductible would leave you unable to cover your other bills, the premium savings may not be worth it.

## Compare like with like

When comparing quotes, use the same deductible for every company so the prices can be compared fairly.
