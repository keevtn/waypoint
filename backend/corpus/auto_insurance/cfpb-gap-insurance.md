# GAP Insurance and Add-on Products

## What GAP covers

Guaranteed Asset Protection (GAP) insurance covers the difference between what you owe on your auto loan and what your insurance pays if the car is stolen or totaled. If you owe more on the loan than the car is worth, you have negative equity, also called being "upside down."

## GAP is generally optional

Generally, you cannot be required to buy an extended warranty, GAP insurance, or credit insurance to get an auto loan. If your loan contract does not require it, the lender cannot insist on it.

## Cost of financing add-ons

Optional add-on products such as GAP that are added into your loan amount increase your total cost, because you borrow more money and pay interest on it. Shop around, including asking your own auto insurer whether it offers GAP coverage.

## Cancelling GAP

If you finance GAP, you generally have the right to cancel it during the term of the loan, which can save you money. If you pay off the loan early, ask about a refund of the unused portion.

## Negative equity from a trade-in

If you roll negative equity from a previous car into a new loan, check whether a GAP plan covers that rolled-over amount; some plans do not.
