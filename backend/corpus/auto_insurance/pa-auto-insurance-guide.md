# Automobile Insurance Guide

## Required coverages in Pennsylvania

Drivers must purchase and maintain auto insurance to legally drive in Pennsylvania. The required coverages are medical benefits, bodily injury liability, and property damage liability. Medical benefits pays the medical bills for you and others covered by your policy, regardless of fault; the minimum limit is $5,000, and higher limits are available. Bodily injury liability pays damages for which you are liable if you injure someone; the minimum limit is $15,000/$30,000, where $15,000 pays for injuries to one person and $30,000 is the total available for one accident. Property damage liability pays for repairs to property you damage when at fault; the minimum limit is $5,000. Some companies offer a combined single limit of $35,000 that meets the bodily injury and property damage minimums. A lapse in coverage may result in suspension of your vehicle registration.

## Limited tort and full tort

If you are in an accident, you are able to recover out-of-pocket medical and certain other expenses under either option. Limited tort offers you significant savings on your premium, but you are not able to recover certain damages, such as payments for pain and suffering, unless the injuries meet one of the exceptions in the law. With full tort, you retain unrestricted rights to bring suit against the negligent party. If you fail to specially elect limited tort, you will be deemed to have elected full tort.

## Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage

These are optional coverages. Uninsured motorist (UM) coverage applies to you, relatives residing in your household, and your passengers if injured by an at-fault uninsured motorist or a hit-and-run driver. Underinsured motorist (UIM) coverage applies if the at-fault motorist does not have enough insurance to pay your claim. Neither covers damage to property.

## Stacking

When you stack UM and/or UIM coverage, your limit for each covered vehicle is multiplied by the number of vehicles on the policy, and you can also combine coverage from more than one policy under which you are insured. By law, stacked coverage applies unless you reject it. You will pay less in premiums if you reject stacking.

## Collision and comprehensive

Collision coverage pays to repair damage to your car as a result of an accident. Comprehensive coverage pays for theft or damage to your car from hazards such as fire, flood, vandalism, and striking an animal. Both are optional under state law. Most banks or lenders require you to buy both coverages to receive a car loan. Under Pennsylvania law, the insurance company applies a $500 collision deductible unless you request a lower amount; comprehensive is offered at various deductible levels. It may not be cost-effective to carry comprehensive or collision coverage on a car worth less than $1,000.

## Deductibles

Choosing higher deductibles on comprehensive and collision coverage may lower your policy cost. The deductible you choose is what you are responsible for paying out of your pocket when you file a claim. Generally speaking, the higher your deductible, the lower your premium.

## GAP coverage

Guaranteed Asset Protection (GAP) pays the difference between the insurance company's payment for a totaled vehicle and the balance of the vehicle loan. It is traditionally purchased when buying a vehicle. GAP does not pay deductibles, missed payments, or late fees, and often has a limit on what it will pay.

## Shopping for a policy

A good rule of thumb is to get quotes from at least three different insurance companies, and make sure the quotes are for the exact same coverages and limits; cost can vary significantly among companies. Ask for copies of the policies and read what is excluded. Most insurers offer discounts for safer drivers, safer cars, multiple policies, and good students. Know the policy term: the most common terms are six months and one year, and shorter terms are re-priced at renewal. Pennsylvania law does not require a grace period for premium payments, so pay on time or the policy can be cancelled.
